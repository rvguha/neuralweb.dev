ARD+ answer ratings

Twenty-four questions in four levels, from one lookup to open analysis. ARD+ breaks each into questions the Neural DB can answer, asks them, and writes an answer from the replies. A second model then grades each answer against those replies.

A addresses the question · G grounded (every figure traces to a reply) · H honest (flags stale, mismatched or missing data) · D decomposition (the sub-questions were the right ones). Each is 0 to 2, graded by Claude Opus against the Neural DB replies, not against an outside truth. Cost includes the Neural DB, the answering model and the grader.
